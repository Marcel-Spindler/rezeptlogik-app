1. Remove from packaging
2. Drain liquid and reserve in hotel pans (10kg per GN 1/1 26.0 Liter)
3. Cut the whole muscle into 2.5 cm pieces and plate 10kg per GN 1/1 26.0 Liter
4. Transfer to production
B. PRODUCTION
1. Place 50 kg of beef in the Hobart mixer with the paddle attachment
2. Add 1 kg of beef jus
3. Start the mixer at 77 rpm for 2 minutes
5. Add 9 kg of beef jus to the shreds and mix gently with your hands. Separate the shreds from the lumps. Add the remaining beef lumps to the new batch to reach another 50 kg.
6. Keep the shreds chilled and reserve for plating
